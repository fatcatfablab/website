About Us

 

We are a 100% volunteer-run NYC makerspace
located in the heart of New York City’s Greenwich Village.

Our mission — to share the magic of making to inspire everyone to grow.

Open to Members 24/7!

Visitors: please only come during our scheduled events

Since 2013, Fat Cat Fab Lab is a hub for artists, students, engineers, hobbyists, startups, and meetups to gather and grow through 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC routing, sewing, electronics, photography, and more.

Volunteer with us!

Everything moves faster with more hands on deck.

  • We need members! We'd love if you, or someone you know wants to be a member!

  • Teach a class! If it’s free, great! If you’d like to make a little, we can work with that too. We’ll provide the space, you bring the class!

  • Learn how to repair equipment to improve our facilities!

  • Donate or help us find equipment we need!

  • Not quite ready to join? Stay informed by subscribing to our newsletter.

  • Attend a class! Check out our Classes & Events and Meetup Events. If you don’t see a class you’re looking for, let us know.

Thank you!

It’s no small task organizing and running a fab lab in New York City! We’re an all volunteer organization run by members. Any extra help is really appreciated.

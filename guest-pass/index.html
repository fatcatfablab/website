Hosting guests at the lab

We love it when members give tours of the lab! It really helps us grow and gets other people excited about what we're doing.

But what about other kinds of visits where you need someome who is not a current member to work or be with you at the lab? 

You can get a day pass for them!

Current Fat Cat Fab Lab members may purchase a Guest Day Pass for a non-member.

Please have them sign a waiver.

Reminder: you are responsible for your guest and should make sure they follow our Rules and Policies.
